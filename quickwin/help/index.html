Start here

Quick Win builds a slot win animation and hands it to After Effects as comps, shape layers, effects and keyframes.

  1. Pick the machine in the band on top: Reels, Cascade or Hold & Spin.

  2. Set up the rounds on the timeline under the picture: what lands, what wins, and when.

  3. Choose what is drawn On screen round a win, and how the spin lands.

  4. Tick the formats at the end of the band, then press After Effects for the package.

Each machine keeps its own rounds, so you can switch between them at any time. Your work is saved in this browser as you go; Save writes a .quickwin.json file with your art in it, and Open brings one back.

Reels

Reels spin and stop in your stop order; each round can win once, on a line, a shape, scatter cells or a cluster.

Reels1 min 5 sec

Quick Win starts with the machine. Reels is for slots whose reels spin and stop, with one win a round.

Set how many reels and rows the board has.

The timeline under the picture holds the rounds. Click a round to work on it.

Pick the symbol that wins this round.

Then its cells. Pick a preset, or click cells here or on the picture.

Type the win. It splits evenly over the cells.

Tease a reel to make it stop last, slowing down and crawling into place.

The spin style and the landing are the same for every round.

The stop order sets which reel stops first.

On screen picks what is drawn round a win: a line, framed cells, and the halo effect.

Win timing plays the winners all together, or one by one.

Play runs every round in a row, each one starting on the board the last one landed.

Drag the white marks on a round to move when its reels stop and its win starts.

Plus Round adds a round after the picked one.

Cascade

Symbols drop in. Winners pop, the rest fall into the gaps and new ones drop in, win after win.

Cascade42 sec

Pick Cascade for games where symbols drop in, and winners pop to make room for new ones.

A cascade round is a chain of tumbles. Each tumble is one win.

Click a tumble to set it up.

Each tumble has its own symbol, cells and win. Pick its cells on the board as the fall before it left it.

Add tumble puts one more win at the end of the chain.

Remove tumble takes out the picked one.

The drop order sets which reel drops in first. The landing sets how a symbol lands.

Pop draws a burst over each winner as it leaves.

Play the round. The board drops in, and the tumbles win one after another.

Tumbles

A round is a chain of tumbles, one tab each. Each has its own winning symbol, cells and win.

Add tumble

One more win at the end of the chain, up to 8 a round.

Remove tumble

Takes out the picked tumble; the ones after it move up.

Winning cells

Picked on the board as the fall before this tumble left it. A note says when cells already sit together.

Drop order

Which reel drops in first.

Pop

A burst over each winner as it leaves the board (On screen).

Note

Quick Win checks that each tumble wins only what you picked: the board is filled so no other win appears by chance.

Hold & Spin

A board of cells: coins land and lock, every respin brings the counter back to three, then the collect counts them into the win.

Hold & Spin52 sec

Pick Hold and Spin for a board of cells, where coins land, lock, and count into the win.

The first round is the start board: the coins that trigger the feature.

Pick a coin, then click cells to place it. The same coin again takes it off.

Each respin spins only the empty cells. The coins already there stay locked.

Place the coins that land on this respin.

Tease picks cells that stop last, crawling in one by one.

When a coin lands, the respins counter goes back to three.

The collect counts every coin into the win, in the stop order. A full board adds the Grand.

Down the right are the coins. Each value is a piece of art with its own Idle and Win comps.

Add a value here, or a new type of coin, and replace any art with your own.

Place

Pick a coin value, a jackpot (Mini, Minor, Major) or Empty, then click cells here or on the picture. The same again takes it off.

Tease

Pick it, then click spinning cells: they stop last, crawling in one by one. Teased cells have a pink ring.

Respins counter

Three dots; back to three when a coin lands. A respin after it runs out is flagged.

Collect

Counts every coin into the win in the stop order. A full board adds the Grand.

Coin art

Down the right: each value and jackpot is its own piece of art with an Idle and a Win comp. Replace puts yours in.

+ Respin, + Value coin, + Jackpot coin

Add a respin after the picked one, or a new type of coin.

Export to After Effects

Tick the formats, press After Effects, and run the script it gives: everything on screen is built from comps, shape layers, effects and keyframes.

Export to After Effects1 min 19 sec

The formats are where it ends. Each one gets its own comps in After Effects.

The check on a tile puts that format in the package, or takes it out.

Click a tile to see that format in the picture.

A tall format can add extra rows under the reels, to fill its height.

Formats with the same rows share their boards, so each board is animated once.

Pick how a board's layers stack: by type, or by symbol, with each winning cell's layers together.

After Effects downloads the package: the script, every symbol's art, and a readme.

Unzip it, and keep the script next to its assets folder.

In After Effects, choose File, Scripts, Run Script File.

Pick the Quick Win script, and open it.

It builds everything in one folder, with a Main comp for each format that plays the whole run.

Each board holds a round's reels, frames, points and effects, all on keyframes.

Built by type, each kind sits together: every point, every frame, every Win comp, every effect.

Built by symbol, each winning cell's point, frame, Win comp and effect sit together, one cell after another.

Every symbol has an Idle and a Win comp.

Animate it there once, and every cell, round and format plays it.

  1. Tick the formats the package builds: 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5.

  2. Press After Effects. A zip downloads with the script, every symbol's art and a readme.

  3. Unzip it. Keep the .jsx next to its assets folder.

  4. In After Effects choose File › Scripts › Run Script File… and pick the .jsx.

What the package builds

One folder, named after your project, with a Main comp per format that plays the whole run.

Symbols (or Coins)

A folder per symbol with its Idle and Win comp, and the shared Win Effect comp. Animate here once: every cell, round and format plays it.

Board, Board +1 rows

A board per round for each row count: the reels on keyframes, the darkening, the win frames, the line, the points and the effects. Formats with the same rows share it.

Counters

The win counter plaque, its number counting up on keys.

16x9 - 1920x1080 (each format)

The format's rounds, its background, and Main, which plays them end to end.

Art

Your pictures, as stills.

Quick Win Controls

Darken %: how dark the losing symbols go during a win, for every round at once.

Text layers use Impact for numbers and points and Arial Bold for labels, faces every Mac and PC has. Each comp carries a comment that says what it is for.

Troubleshooting

The few things that can go wrong, and what to do.

After Effects says pictures were not found

The script reads the art from the assets folder next to it. Unzip the package first, keep the .jsx and assets together, and run it again.

The win effect is too bright or too soft

Open the Win Effect comp and change the two Glows on its Bloom layer. Every winner uses this one comp.

A round says it is too short

Teased reels and tumbles need time. Make the round longer, or drag its marks.

MP4 preview does not start

It needs a browser that encodes video (a recent Chrome, Edge or Safari). The After Effects package works in any browser.
